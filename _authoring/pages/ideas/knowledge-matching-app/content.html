Concept

A past concept for transforming personal contact networks into a trusted marketplace for questions, requests and useful introductions.

Why it matters

It explored how the right answer or opportunity is often only one or two trusted relationships away, but hard to surface deliberately.

How it could work

The experience imagined people sharing a request through selected contacts and rewarding the chain of trust when a useful match was made.
