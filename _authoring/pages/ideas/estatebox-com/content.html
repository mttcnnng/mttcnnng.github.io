EstateBox.com

A past concept for gathering estate records, wishes and instructions in one trusted place.

Concept

A past concept for a secure estate-planning vault that gathered important records, wishes and instructions in one trusted place.

Why it matters

It addressed the stress families and executors face when essential information is scattered, missing or hard to access at the wrong moment.

How it could work

The experience imagined a person preparing a clear estate box for trusted representatives, covering assets, contacts, wishes and practical instructions.
