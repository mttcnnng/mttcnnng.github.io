LiquiPak

A past concept for mobile drink vendors serving crowds without fixed queues.

Concept

A past concept for mobile vendors carrying backpack-mounted beverage dispensers at venues, events and busy public spaces.

Why it matters

It aimed to reduce queues and missed moments by bringing refreshments directly to people instead of making people leave the action.

How it could work

The experience imagined vendors moving through crowds with hot or cold drinks, serving customers quickly wherever demand appeared.

Status

Past idea from 2001.
