ClickNowPayLater.com

A past concept for helping web retailers offer Buy Now, Pay Later at checkout.

Concept

A past concept for letting web retailers offer Buy Now, Pay Later at checkout without pulling shoppers away from the purchase flow.

Why it matters

It explored a way for customers to receive goods immediately while spreading the cost into more manageable monthly repayments.

How it could work

The experience imagined shoppers choosing a pay-later option at checkout, seeing a clear repayment plan and completing the order in one familiar flow.
